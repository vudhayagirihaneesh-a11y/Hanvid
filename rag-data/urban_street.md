# Urban and Street Scenes

- **Night Neon**: 'rain-slicked city street at night, neon signs reflecting in puddles, magenta and cyan glow, cinematic anamorphic look.'
- **Daytime Street**: 'bustling street market in late afternoon sun, long shadows, people browsing stalls, telephoto compression.'
- **Skyline Establishing**: 'wide establishing shot of the skyline at blue hour, building lights beginning to turn on.'
- **Timelapse**: 'hyperlapse through the downtown core at dusk' — motion energy without fast subject movement.
- **Rooftop / Drone**: 'drone shot rising above the rooftops revealing the grid of the city stretching to the horizon.'
- **Crowd Control**: keep crowds in the mid-background ('blurred pedestrians passing in the foreground') — dense moving crowds up close smear and distort.
- **Traffic**: 'light trails from passing cars, long exposure at night' reads cleaner than individual moving vehicles.

Urban scenes need a light source story: neon, streetlamps, headlights, shop windows.
