# Horror, Suspense & Low-Key Chiaroscuro
## 1. Chiaroscuro Lighting & Shadow Dominance
- High-Contrast Shadow: 'Deep crushed obsidian shadows consuming 80% of the frame, single harsh tungsten flashlight beam cutting through cold damp air.'
- Obscured Subjects: 'Eerily tall silhouette lingering at the end of a dilapidated hallway, backlit by a flickering sodium-vapor security light.'
## 2. Psychological Camera Techniques
- Unsettling Kinematics: 'Slow creeping Dutch-angle push-in, suffocatingly tight framing leaving the negative space behind the character uncomfortably exposed.'
- Atmosphere: 'Slowly peeling wallpaper, condensing condensation on cold cellar pipes, drifting mold spore particles in the flashlight beam.'
