# Accurate Humans: Faces, Hands and Emotion

When generating people, prevent anatomical disturbances:

- **Anatomy Keywords**: 'anatomically correct, clear facial features, symmetrical face, natural proportions, realistic hands.'
- **Distance**: Medium shots and close-ups resolve faces far better than extreme wides where detail collapses.
- **Lighting**: 'soft studio lighting' or 'even diffused light' helps the model render facial features properly. Harsh contrast hides structure and invites artifacts.
- **Hands**: Keep hands relaxed and partially visible ('hands resting on the table'). Complex hand actions (typing, gripping tools) are the #1 anatomy failure — avoid or crop them out.
- **Emotion via Face and Posture**: 'warm genuine smile, relaxed shoulders, direct eye contact' — describe the expression, not the abstract feeling.
- **One Person Per Clip**: Multi-person interaction often merges bodies or swaps faces. Generate individuals, cut them together.
- **Facing**: 'facing the camera' is stable; extreme profile and back-of-head shots are where identity drifts.

Eyes and mouth sell realism — give them light and moderate motion.
