# Camera Movements and Angles

To make a video dynamic, describe the camera's behavior explicitly — the model cannot guess motion intent.

- **Tracking Shot**: 'Camera tracks alongside the running subject.'
- **Dolly In / Push In**: 'Camera slowly pushes in toward the subject's face.'
- **Drone Shot / Aerial View**: 'Aerial drone footage sweeping over the forest.'
- **Dutch Angle**: 'Tilted camera angle showing unease.'
- **Low Angle**: 'Low angle looking up at the imposing character, making them look powerful.'
- **High Angle**: 'High angle looking down, making the subject appear small.'
- **Rack Focus**: 'Focus shifts from the foreground object to the background character.'
- **Slow Motion**: 'Slow motion, 120fps, highly detailed.'
- **Static Tripod Shot**: 'Locked-off tripod shot, perfectly stable framing.'

Use ONE camera behavior per clip. Combining several camera moves in a single prompt confuses the model and produces warping.
