# Documentary and Realism Style

For factual, grounded, true-to-life footage:

- **Natural Light**: 'natural daylight', 'overcast soft light', 'available light indoors'. Avoid stylized lighting words.
- **Camera Language**: 'handheld documentary camera, subtle natural sway', 'observational static shot from a distance'.
- **Interview Framing**: 'medium close-up, subject seated, soft window light from the left, looking slightly off-camera'.
- **B-Roll Patterns**: 'hands working at a workshop bench, close-up detail', 'wide shot of the village at dawn', 'slow pan across the market stalls'.
- **Realism Keywords**: 'photojournalistic, candid, unposed, true-to-life colors, natural skin tones'.
- **Avoid**: glossy commercial words ('hero shot', 'luxury'), heavy color grades, and perfect symmetry — real scenes are imperfect by design.

Documentary prompts should read like a shot list a camera operator could actually film.
