# Technical Formats and Resolutions

Match the technical language to the deliverable:

- **Aspect Ratios**: '16:9 widescreen' for YouTube and web. '9:16 vertical' for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. '2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen' for a cinematic letterboxed look. '1:1 square' for social feeds.
- **Resolutions**: 480p renders fastest and most stably; 720p is the sweet spot for quality; 1080p for final delivery. Lower resolution first to test a prompt, then re-run the good ones at higher resolution.
- **Frame Rates**: '24fps' looks cinematic, '30fps' looks like standard video, 'slow motion 120fps' for dramatic speed ramps. Do not ask for frame rates the model cannot deliver — describe the LOOK ('smooth slow motion') instead.
- **Duration**: Generated clips are ~5 seconds. Plan sequences as: number of shots × 5 seconds.

Describe the intended delivery format in the prompt so framing matches: a '9:16 vertical' prompt composes differently than a wide one.
