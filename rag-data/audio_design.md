# Audio and Sound Design Pairing

The service generates synchronized audio from the video prompt, so prompt with sound in mind:

- **Name the Ambience**: 'quiet forest with birdsong', 'busy café chatter', 'waves on a pebble shore' — environmental sound words steer both picture and audio coherently.
- **Name Key Sound Events**: one clear sound moment per clip: 'espresso machine hissing', 'rain drumming on the umbrella', 'engine revving'.
- **Mood Words Carry Music**: 'uplifting', 'melancholic', 'tense', 'serene' guide the musical bed better than genre names.
- **Match Audio to Motion**: slow visuals want sustained pads; dynamic cuts want percussive hits. 'Slow motion' + 'ethereal ambient tone' is a reliable pairing.
- **Avoid Impossible Sync**: do not ask for lip-synced dialogue — generated speech does not match mouth movement.

Write the prompt so a sound designer could read it and build the track: environment, one sound event, one mood.
