# Retro Synthwave, Cybergrid & VHS Aesthetics
## 1. Analog CRT & Tape Artifacts
- VHS Tape Texture: 'Subtle horizontal tracking lines, phosphor glow bloom on bright elements, magnetic tape noise, authentic tape dropouts along the bottom margin.'
- Color Bleed: 'NTSC composite video color crosstalk, red/cyan chroma phase shifting on high-contrast vertical edges.'
## 2. Synthwave Landscape Motifs
- Wireframe Grids: 'Glowing purple and cyan perspective vector grid undulating over digital hills into an oversized setting neon wireframe sun.'
- Automotive Icon: 'DeLorean or Testarossa driving toward the horizon, taillights drawing persistent laser ribbons across the dark cybergrid.'
