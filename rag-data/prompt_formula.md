# The Professional Prompt Formula

Professional video prompts follow a fixed order. Fill every slot, drop the slots that do not apply:

**[SHOT TYPE] of [SUBJECT with 2-3 visual attributes] [ONE action] in [SETTING] during [TIME/WEATHER], [LIGHTING], [CAMERA BEHAVIOR], [STYLE], [QUALITY].**

Worked example:

'Medium shot of a young woman in a red raincoat with wet hair, smiling as she looks up at the falling rain, on a quiet city street at dusk, warm streetlamp glow with soft rim light, static tripod shot, cinematic teal and orange grade, highly detailed, sharp focus, smooth stable motion.'

Slot-by-slot:

1. **SHOT TYPE**: close-up / medium / wide / aerial.
2. **SUBJECT + ATTRIBUTES**: age, clothing, colors — the identity anchors.
3. **ONE ACTION**: a single verb the subject performs.
4. **SETTING + TIME**: place, hour, weather.
5. **LIGHTING**: quality and direction.
6. **CAMERA**: behavior and lens.
7. **STYLE**: film genre, grade, animation medium.
8. **QUALITY**: sharpness and stability keywords.

A prompt that fills every slot beats a longer prompt that fills them randomly.
