# Food and Beverage Videography

- **Macro Texture**: 'extreme macro of melting butter on a fresh pancake stack, steam rising, shallow depth of field.' Texture = appetite.
- **The Pour**: 'slow-motion pour of coffee into a glass cup, crema swirling, backlit' — pours are the most reliable food motion.
- **Steam and Freshness**: 'wisps of steam rising from the bowl, backlit against a dark background' signals hot and fresh.
- **Overhead Flat Lay**: 'top-down flat lay of ingredients arranged on a marble counter, soft even light' — the recipe-video standard.
- **Warm Light Always**: 'warm side light, dark moody background, rustic wooden table' — food dies under cold blue light.
- **Process Shots**: 'hands kneading dough on a flour-dusted counter, flour particles catching the light, close-up.'
- **Hero Plate**: 'chef places the finished plate down, steam rising, subtle orbit around the plate.'

Keep one dish per clip, camera close, motion minimal — food should glisten, not sprint.
