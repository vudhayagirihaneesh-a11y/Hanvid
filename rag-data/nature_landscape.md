# Nature and Landscape Videography

- **Golden Hour First**: 'golden hour sunlight raking across the hillside, long warm shadows' — nature looks most cinematic at the edges of the day.
- **Weather as Mood**: 'morning mist in the valley', 'dramatic storm clouds gathering', 'fresh rain on leaves with droplets catching light'. Weather adds motion for free.
- **Aerial Establishing**: 'sweeping aerial drone shot flying over the coastline, revealing the bay', 'top-down drone view of the winding river'.
- **Scale Reference**: 'tiny hiker on the vast ridge for scale' — pure empty landscapes lack a focal anchor.
- **Wildlife Distance**: 'telephoto shot of a deer at the forest edge, 400mm lens compression, shallow depth of field' — never prompt animals up close with a wide lens; it distorts anatomy.
- **Water Motion**: 'long exposure smooth water' vs 'crashing waves frozen mid-air' — specify which look.
- **Seasonal Cues**: 'autumn foliage in red and gold', 'spring meadow in bloom' lock the palette naturally.

Keep camera motion slow and majestic; nature footage moves at glacier pace.
