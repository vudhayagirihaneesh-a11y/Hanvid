# Shot Types and Composition

Name the shot size explicitly — it controls how much detail the model renders.

- **Extreme Close-Up (ECU)**: 'Extreme close-up of the eye, every detail visible.' Maximum detail, use for emotion.
- **Close-Up (CU)**: 'Close-up portrait, face fills the frame.'
- **Medium Shot (MS)**: 'Medium shot, subject from the waist up.' Best balance for faces and action.
- **Wide Shot (WS)**: 'Wide shot showing the subject and the environment.'
- **Extreme Wide / Establishing**: 'Extreme wide establishing shot, tiny figure in a vast landscape.'

Composition rules the model understands:

- **Rule of Thirds**: 'Subject positioned on the left third of the frame.'
- **Leading Lines**: 'Composition with strong leading lines drawing the eye to the subject.'
- **Negative Space**: 'Generous negative space around the subject.'
- **Headroom**: 'Balanced headroom, eyes on the upper third line.'

Faces render most accurately in close-ups and medium shots; extreme wide shots lose facial detail.
