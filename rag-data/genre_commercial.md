# Commercial and Product Style

For advertising-grade product and brand footage:

- **Hero Shot**: 'premium product hero shot, product centered on a seamless background, soft studio key light with subtle rim highlight.'
- **Slow Orbit**: 'camera slowly orbits the product, 180 degrees, perfectly smooth motion.' The signature commercial move.
- **Macro Detail**: 'extreme macro detail shot revealing surface texture, shallow depth of field.'
- **Clean Environment**: 'minimalist studio background, gradient backdrop, no distractions' or 'styled set with soft props'.
- **Lighting Setup**: 'three-point studio lighting, large softbox key, gentle fill, crisp specular highlights' — commercial looks are defined by controlled light.
- **Premium Keywords**: 'luxury advertising aesthetic, high-end commercial, pristine, flawless surfaces, glossy reflections.'
- **Lifestyle Cut**: 'product in real use, natural morning light, authentic hands' — pairs with the hero shot in an edit.

Keep motion minimal and deliberate; products must stay perfectly sharp and structurally intact.
