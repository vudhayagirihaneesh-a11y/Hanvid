# Depth of Field and Focus

Depth of field (DOF) is the strongest 'professional camera' signal you can put in a prompt.

- **Shallow DOF**: 'Shallow depth of field, f/1.8, creamy bokeh background, subject in razor-sharp focus.' Isolates the subject — the default look of commercials and interviews.
- **Deep Focus**: 'Deep focus, everything sharp from foreground to background.' Used in landscapes and classic cinema.
- **Rack Focus**: 'Focus pulls from the blurred foreground flower to the sharp subject behind.' One focus change per clip only.
- **Macro**: 'Extreme macro shot, tiny details filling the frame, shallow depth of field.'
- **Lens Language**: '85mm portrait lens, compressed background', '24mm wide angle, slight distortion at the edges', 'anamorphic lens, oval bokeh, horizontal lens flare.'

Warning: asking for 'blurry background' AND 'everything in focus' in the same prompt produces conflicting, smeared output. Choose one.
