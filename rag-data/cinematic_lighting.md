# Cinematic Lighting for Video Generation

When prompting for live-action or cinematic scenes, use these keywords to enhance realism:

- **Volumetric Lighting**: Adds dust motes and light rays. Use 'god rays' or 'volumetric lighting'.
- **Chiaroscuro**: High contrast between light and dark.
- **Neon Noir**: Cyberpunk style, neon reflections on wet streets, magenta and cyan hues.
- **Golden Hour**: Warm, soft lighting right before sunset.
- **Blue Hour**: Cool twilight tones just after sunset.
- **Rim Lighting**: A bright outline around the subject to separate them from the background.
- **Soft Diffused Light**: 'Soft diffused lighting, gentle shadows' for flattering, even exposure.
- **Practical Lights**: Visible lamps, candles or screens inside the frame that motivate the light.

Lighting is the single strongest realism cue. Always name the light quality: 'soft', 'harsh', 'warm', 'cool', 'directional'.
